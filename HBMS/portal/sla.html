SERVICE COMMITMENTS

Vendor SLA management

Response and resolution are tracked as separate commitments.

Response time

Elapsed time from breakdown report to first recorded acknowledgement or response.

Resolution time

Elapsed time from report to repair completion and verification.

Critical-care escalation

Critical-care reports without a recorded response are escalated when their four-hour response deadline passes.

The four-hour rule applies to response, not guaranteed repair completion. The operational SLA ledger is available in the desktop client/database.
