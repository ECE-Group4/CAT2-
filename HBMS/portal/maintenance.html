ENGINEERING WORKFLOW

Maintenance activity

A controlled workflow from breakdown report to verified return to service.

  1. Report and triageCapture asset, location, severity and fault details.
  2. Assign and respondAssign an engineer and record the first response time.
  3. Diagnose and repairRecord diagnosis, work performed, parts used and repair costs.
  4. Verify and closeRecord verification evidence and close only after the equipment is checked.
